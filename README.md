# AI Powered Recipe Builder 🥘
For all leftovers ingredients we have in our fridge, what we should do about them? This app is just what we need for it!

# How it works 🤖
1.) Insert the leftovers ingredients you have\
2.) Let AI suggests you a recipe to save your time thinking
